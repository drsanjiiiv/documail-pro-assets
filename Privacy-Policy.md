# Privacy Policy for DocuMail Pro

Your privacy is a top priority. DocuMail Pro handles data strictly in compliance with Google API User Data Policies.

1. **Data Access & Scope:** The add-on requests access to scopes like Google Sheets, Google Documents, Google Drive, and Gmail solely to execute your configured automation tasks (e.g., pulling row data, generating PDFs, and sending emails on your behalf).
2. **Data Storage:** DocuMail Pro operates entirely client-side or within your isolated Google Apps Script environment. We do not transmit, collect, or store your spreadsheet data, email text, or personal information on external third-party servers. No user data is stored by the add-on itself; all data remains inside your own Google Workspace files.
3. **Third-Party Sharing:** No user data is ever shared, sold, or used for advertising purposes. All data operations remain localized to your active Google Workspace ecosystem. User data is used solely to provide the mail-merge and document-generation features visible in the add-on interface, and is never used to create, train, or improve any AI/ML model.
4. **Limited Use Disclosure:** The use of information received from Google Workspace scopes will adhere to the Google User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

For details on managing or deleting your data, please see the DocuMail Pro support documentation.
